The Art of Pure Flavor

The Prime American Blend

Short rib, brisket, and dry-aged chuck sourced exclusively from family-owned ranches in Nebraska and Texas. Ground fresh twice daily at 34°F to preserve fat crystallization.

Seared on 700° White Oak

Smashed on screaming-hot custom steel plates over natural hardwood embers. This triggers the ultimate Maillard reaction: a lace-crisp crust that seals in natural juices.

Slow-Proofed Potato Brioche

Baked fresh every morning with clarified butter and Yukon Gold potato flour for cloud-like softness that compresses gracefully without ever turning soggy.

Born in New York.
Perfected in Texas.

SAMPLE began as an underground culinary experiment in a Lower East Side alleyway. Our singular mission: strip away the frozen patties, artificial stabilizers, and unnecessary restaurant fluff.

Today, our open-fire hearths in Manhattan and Austin smoke burgers daily over white oak embers with genuine American craft.
